# Code of conduct

OpenChar is used and built by people in many countries and many lines of
work: plant operators, farmers' organisations, lab staff, verifiers,
researchers and software developers. Many of them write in a second language.

We expect everyone taking part in the project to be respectful and patient,
to assume good faith, and to keep criticism about the work rather than the
person. Harassment, insults and discrimination of any kind are not accepted.

We follow the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

To report a problem, contact the maintainers privately through the
repository's **Security** tab (**Report a vulnerability** reaches only the
maintainers), or by direct message to a maintainer. Reports are handled in
confidence.
